Expensy is a privacy-focused, fully offline personal finance and expense manager built with Material You design.

Features:
- Multi-account management (Cash, Bank, Credit & Debit Cards, Savings, Investments, Digital Wallets, Physical Gold)
- Categories & Subcategories with two-tier quick selection, parent budget roll-up, and drill-down reports
- Split transactions, recurring payments & installments with background auto-pay
- Time deposits & certificates, loans, lending/borrowing tracker, wishlist & savings goals
- Insights, statistics, financial calendar, Expensy Wrapped, Excel & PDF reports
- Encrypted (.enc) and JSON backups, selective restore, scheduled auto-backup, and TrackWallet CSV / GreenStash migration
- Material You dynamic colors, pitch-black AMOLED mode, and 12+ languages
